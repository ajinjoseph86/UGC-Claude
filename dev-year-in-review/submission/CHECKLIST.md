# ElevenLabs "Year of More" competition: submission checklist

Brief: https://join.elevenlabs.io/year-of-more · Rules: https://join.elevenlabs.io/year-of-more/terms
**Final deadline: Sun 30 Nov 2026, 11:59pm US Eastern time.** One $10,000 prize per day, so post early.

## Before posting
- [ ] **Audio rule.** Audio must be generated through your own ElevenLabs account; anything else needs a licence. Swap or mute the non-ElevenLabs sound: the Higgsfield clip voices (e.g. "this is so easy") and the synthesised clicks and chimes.
- [x] Watermark: ElevenCreative logo bottom-right, about ¼ of the video width (16:9), visible for the whole video, not covered.
- [ ] No "GTA" anywhere (video, title, caption, form). Use "West Coast G-funk / LA crime-comedy".
- [ ] Not posted anywhere before the contest posts.
- [ ] No real brands, logos or real people. (Characters are AI originals; NINJUTSKU and the chat app are made up.)

## Posting (both on the same day, US Eastern time)
- [ ] Two platforms from your own **public** accounts (Instagram / TikTok / YouTube incl. Shorts / X / LinkedIn)
- [ ] Caption includes **#morethanvoice** and **#ElevenCreativeCompetitionVol2**
- [ ] Tag **@elevencreative** and **@elevenlabs** on both posts
- [ ] Turn on each platform's AI-generated content label
- [ ] Keep both posts public until **31 Jan 2027**; no paid boosting or bought engagement

## Within 24 hours of the second post
- [ ] Entry form: https://forms.gle/AqrWgEAxQzhqJQ1K9
  - Legal name, **the email on your ElevenLabs account**, country, handles, both post links
  - **Which ElevenCreative features you used and how** (Use of ElevenCreative is 50% of the score)

## Judging
- Use of ElevenCreative: 50% (depth and integration beyond voice)
- Creativity: 30%
- Craft and polish: 20%

## Proof
- [ ] Keep the ElevenLabs flows and generation history. Don't delete them, because finalists must prove how the work was generated.
- Main flow: "Dev's Year in Review — v2 video" (video parts, room image, music cues and scores)
- Dialogue flow: "Dev's Year in Review — dialogue"

## Limits
- One entry per day, up to 15 in total, each a different video. No multiple accounts.
